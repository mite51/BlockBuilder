Use case: stylized-concept
Create a square stylized 3D game asset turnaround sheet: a clean 2x2 grid of
four camera views of ONE IDENTICAL PHYSICAL chunky cube-shaped terrain block.
Use the accepted preview as the identity reference, preserving its shape,
palette, material features and sculpted style.

MATERIAL
{{description}}

Hand-painted cartoon appearance, broad irregular sculpted forms, softly rounded
corners, shallow readable cracks/pits appropriate to the material, and sparse
fine detail. Small clods, stones or roots may protrude while the silhouette
stays clearly cubic. Smooth shading, predominantly matte surfaces, crisp
sculpted detail, and a polished 3D game aesthetic. Keep texture chunky and
readable rather than densely granular; adapt gloss/translucency for the material.

VIEW LAYOUT — these are instructions only, NEVER printed labels:
- Upper left: slightly elevated front-right three-quarter view; top, front,
  and right vertical faces visible.
- Upper right: slightly elevated front-left three-quarter view; top, front,
  and left vertical faces visible.
- Lower left: LOW rear-left three-quarter view, camera BELOW the upright
  block looking upward; clearly reveal the BOTTOM, back and left faces.
- Lower right: LOW rear-right three-quarter view, camera BELOW the upright
  block looking upward; clearly reveal the BOTTOM, back and right faces.

CONSISTENCY
These are views of the same physical asset. Every shared face must retain
exactly the same clod shapes, stones, roots, cracks, colors and surface details
in the same positions. Do not mirror or independently redesign any face.
Features crossing edges connect coherently. Top matches between upper views;
bottom matches between lower views; front matches between upper views; back
matches between lower views; left and right faces match their corresponding
upper/lower views. Keep the block upright and move the camera below it for
the lower views; do not draw the top where the bottom belongs.

Soft even studio lighting with enough fill to keep the underside visible.
Pure white background, NO cast shadows, contact shadows, floor shadows,
ground plane or background gradients. All four blocks are the same scale,
evenly spaced in equal-sized cells, with generous white outer AND internal
margins so a center split will not cut the objects.
ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT, VIEW LABELS, PANEL BORDERS, ARROWS OR WATERMARKS.
