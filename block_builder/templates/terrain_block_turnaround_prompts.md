# Terrain block four-view generation prompts

23 standalone prompts based on the original Coarse Dirt turnaround specification. Muddy Mangrove Roots and Snow Layers are excluded. Copy one complete prompt at a time.

## Coarse Dirt

```text
A stylized 3D game asset turnaround sheet showing four consistent views of the same chunky, cube-shaped block of warm light-brown earth, arranged in a clean 2×2 grid on a square canvas.

The block is made of compacted dirt with broad, irregular sculpted clumps, softly rounded corners, shallow cracks, small pits, and a sparse scattering of tiny soil granules. Larger clods gently protrude around the edges while preserving a clear cubic silhouette. Rounded tan and ochre stones are embedded in the soil, with a few tiny pale-grey pebbles for variation. The top and bottom have the same earthy material. Hand-painted cartoon appearance, predominantly matte surfaces, smooth shading, crisp sculpted detail, and a polished 3D game aesthetic. Keep the texture readable and chunky rather than densely granular. Keep the texture readable and chunky rather than densely granular. No grass or plants.

View layout:

- MAIN — upper left: Slightly elevated front-right three-quarter view, clearly showing the top and two adjoining vertical faces (front and right).
- LEFT — upper right: Slightly elevated front-left three-quarter view, showing the top and the front and left vertical faces.
- BACK — lower left: Low rear-left three-quarter view, with the camera below the block looking slightly upward to clearly reveal the bottom and the back and left vertical faces.
- RIGHT — lower right: Low rear-right three-quarter view, with the camera below the block looking slightly upward to clearly reveal the bottom and the back and right vertical faces.

Consistency is essential: These are four camera views of one identical physical asset. Every shared face must retain exactly the same sculpted shapes, material patterns, embedded features, cracks, colors, and surface details in the same positions as the camera rotates. Any features crossing an edge must connect coherently onto the adjoining face. Do not mirror or independently redesign the faces. The top must match between MAIN and LEFT; the bottom must match between BACK and RIGHT; the front must match between MAIN and LEFT; the back must match between BACK and RIGHT; the left must match between LEFT and BACK; the right must match between MAIN and RIGHT. Keep the asset upright in all views; move the camera below it for the two lower views so the underside is genuinely visible.

Use soft, even studio lighting with gentle shading to describe the form and enough fill light to keep the undersides visible. Pure white background with no cast shadows, contact shadows, floor shadows, or background gradients.

Keep all four blocks at the same scale, evenly spaced, with generous white margins. Place each view's label in bold, dark-grey sans-serif lettering centered beneath its block without overlapping the asset. Exact labels: MAIN, LEFT, BACK, RIGHT. No panel borders, arrows, or additional text.
```
